## 📂 Featured Projects

### 🧪 Arsenic Removal Using Modified PAN Fiber

**Final Year Project**

Synthesis and characterization of chemically modified Polyacrylonitrile (PAN) fiber for arsenic removal from synthetic water.

- Developed modified PAN fibers for arsenic adsorption
- Characterized modifications using FTIR and SEM
- Conducted fixed-bed column experiments under different operating conditions
- Achieved **98.5% arsenic removal efficiency** using modified PAN fiber

### ⚙️ Centrifugal Pump Simulation Using Aspen HYSYS

Simulation-based performance and energy efficiency analysis of a centrifugal pump under varying operating conditions.

- Developed a centrifugal pump simulation in Aspen HYSYS
- Studied the effects of flow rate, temperature, discharge pressure, and pump efficiency
- Evaluated pump head, power consumption, and specific energy consumption
- Conducted **21 simulation cases**
